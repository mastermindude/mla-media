How the Rule Works

One family.
Two legal outcomes.

The second-generation cut-off is easiest to understand when the rule is visible instead of buried in legal wording.

6(1)

Can transmit with one entitled parent

A person registered under section 6(1) can transmit entitlement even when the other parent is not entitled.

6(2)

Needs another entitled parent

A person registered under section 6(2) can transmit entitlement only when the other parent is also entitled (Indigenous Services Canada).

Three generations

The family remains.
The legal line can end.

Generation One

6(1) grandparent

  • Grandparent registered under 6(1)
  • Other grandparent not entitled
  • Their child is entitled under 6(2)
Generation Two

6(2) parent

  • Parent registered under 6(2)
  • Other parent not entitled
  • Their child is not entitled
Generation Three

Legal line ends

  • The registration connection stops
  • The family relationship remains

After two consecutive generations of parenting with someone who is not entitled, the third generation may no longer qualify for registration (Indigenous Services Canada).
