The Law Now

The rule may change.
It has not changed yet.

Current as of October 14, 2026. The proposed one-parent rule is still a proposal, not current registration law.

1985
Bill C-31

Bill C-31 introduced the general 6(1) and 6(2) structure associated with the second-generation cut-off (Indigenous Services Canada).

2025
Senate amendment

In December 2025, the Senate amended Bill S-2 to include a proposed remedy to the second-generation cut-off through a one-parent rule (Indigenous Services Canada).

2026
Still in committee

On October 14, 2026, Parliament lists Bill S-2 as under consideration by the Standing Committee on Indigenous and Northern Affairs in the House of Commons. The proposed change is not yet law (Parliament of Canada).

Changing the formula matters. The larger question remains. Why should a distant federal system decide who belongs to a First Nation?
